Who We Are · 01

Bridging
hemp
supply and
demand.

Vantis International Group is a specialized brokerage connecting verified industrial hemp and bio-polymer suppliers with manufacturers across automotive, construction, packaging, and textile industries. We don't manufacture — we broker, vet, and de-risk the trade, holding select buffer stock on high-velocity materials to close timing gaps between harvest and demand.

The right material, from the right supplier, at the right price — without the friction of fragmented global supply chains.

From sourcing and specification matching to logistics and settlement, Vantis manages the full transaction lifecycle so buyers and sellers can focus on what they do best.
